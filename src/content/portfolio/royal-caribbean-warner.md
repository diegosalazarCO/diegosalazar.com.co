Worked on a tvOS app for Turner/WarnerMedia, mainly for the TNT and TBS brands, using Obj-C and Swift 5.

Also worked on a chat/messaging project with Royal Caribbean Cruises Ltd. The chat app is used to communicate inside the ship — sending and receiving messages, adding friends, and more. Built with the XMPP Framework, Clean Architecture and Swift 5.
