Antezala is a B2B2C tool aimed at independent wedding planners and couples to visualize real, empty venues photorealistically dressed for a wedding before committing to anything.

Upload a photo of an empty venue, and Antezala generates a photorealistic render of that exact space dressed for ceremony or reception — keeping the same architecture, light, and surroundings.

Key architectural concepts:
- **Parameterisation over prompting**: Structured UI inputs compile into precise edit instructions so results stay consistent without prompt drift.
- **Geometry before generation**: Layout blocks are composed in pixel space to guarantee correct scale and placement before diffusion.
- **Regional plausibility**: Flora and decor adapt dynamically to the venue's region and season.
